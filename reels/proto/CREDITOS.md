# Créditos dos dados de movimento

The motion capture data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
(CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database: free for research and commercial use; the creators ask that the source be credited.)

Clips usados: 02_03 (run/jog), 127_13 e 127_14 (run side step). Ficheiro derivado: mocap_cycles.json.
